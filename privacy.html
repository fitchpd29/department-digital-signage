Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Purpose

Department Digital Signage is an internal application used to distribute department-managed MP4 videos to authorized digital display computers.

Google Drive Access

The application uses Google Drive authorization to retrieve video files designated for display. Access is limited to authorized department systems.

Data Handling

Downloaded videos are stored locally on authorized computers for playback. Authentication credentials are stored on the authorized computers and are not published on this website.

Data Sharing

The application does not sell Google user data or intentionally share it with advertisers.

Revoking Access

Authorized Google account holders can revoke application access through their Google Account security settings.
